Troubleshooting Common Problems

If your home energy upgrades are acting up, these are the most common issues and how to fix them.

  • → Smart thermostat not learning — HVAC system incompatible or wiring issue
  • → LED bulbs flickering on dimmer — check compatibility and replace with dimmable-rated bulbs
  • → Insulation trapping moisture — add vapor barrier or switch to open-cell spray foam

Need more help? Check out the full Troubleshooting Hub for a complete list of common energy efficiency problems and solutions.

Quick Reference: Energy Efficiency Basics

  • Free upgrades:LED bulbs, weatherstripping, smart thermostat scheduling — save $100-300/year combined
  • Mid-range ($500-2k):Attic insulation (R-38), window film, tankless water heater — payback 3-7 years
  • Long-term ($10k+):Solar panels, heat pump HVAC, whole-home air sealing — payback 5-12 years with incentives
  • Average US home energy use:~10,649 kWh/year (~$1,372/year at national avg rate of $0.129/kWh)
  • Best ROI upgrade:Attic insulation (R-38 to R-60) — typically pays back in 2-4 years
