Troubleshooting Common Problems

If your BSF system is acting up, these are the most common issues and how to fix them.

  • → No larvae appearing — attractant too weak, temperature below 70°F, or bin not exposed to adults
  • → Too many larvae — reduce feeding rate or harvest frass more frequently
  • → Unpleasant odor — food waste too wet, insufficient airflow, or larvae buried under surface layer

Need more help? Check out the full Troubleshooting Hub for a complete list of common BSF problems and solutions.

Quick Reference: Black Soldier Fly Basics

  • Larval consumption rate:Can eat their body weight in waste per day (~5g/day per larva)
  • Life cycle duration:Egg to adult: 4-6 weeks; larval stage: 10-14 days at optimal temperature
  • Optimal temperature:75-90°F (24-32°C) for fastest growth; below 60°F larvae become inactive
  • Frass NPK range:~3-1-2 to 5-2-3 depending on diet; rich in beneficial microbes and chitinase enzymes
  • Adult lifespan:5-8 days (no mouthparts — they don't eat as adults, only breed)
