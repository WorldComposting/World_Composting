World Composting

Turning waste into wonder.

Whether you're a beginner looking to reduce household waste or an experienced gardener chasing the perfect compost, World Composting is your complete resource. Explore in-depth guides, step-by-step checklists, curated video playlists, and trusted gear recommendations, all designed to help you master composting at any level.

What I cover: From worm bins under the kitchen sink to backyard hot-compost piles that reach 160°F, from bokashi fermentation in sealed buckets to biochar-amended soil that holds moisture for weeks. My guides break down every major composting method into clear, actionable steps. I explain why each technique works (the science of thermophilic bacteria, the carbon-to-nitrogen balance, the role of red wigglers) so you can troubleshoot problems before they start.

Who it's for: Apartment dwellers with no yard space, suburban gardeners building their first pile, urban homesteaders scaling up to farm-size operations, and anyone who wants to turn kitchen scraps into nutrient-rich soil instead of sending them to a landfill. No prior experience required; my checklists start at "Day 1" and walk you through every phase.

My mission: To make composting accessible, practical, and rewarding for everyone. I believe that turning waste into fertile soil is one of the most impactful things a household can do for the environment, and it doesn't have to be complicated or smelly. Every guide on this site is written from real-world experience: years of hands-on vermicomposting, hot-compost pile management, and testing dozens of gear products so you don't have to.

Why trust me: I've been composting since 2015, across worm bins, bokashi buckets, tumblers, and open-air piles. My YouTube channel (World Composting) has helped over 50,000 viewers start their own systems. Almost every product recommendation on this site is tested in my own bins first. I only link to gear I've personally used or verified through independent reviews.

What you'll find here: Long-form guides that actually walk you through a method from start to finish, interactive checklists with progress tracking so you never lose your place, deep-dive articles on the stuff that trips people up (fruit flies, C:N ratios, why your pile won't heat), a glossary of composting terms when a word puzzles you, troubleshooting pages for common problems, YouTube playlists organized by method, and a gear page with links to products I've actually tested. Everything is free, no ads, and updated as new methods come along.
