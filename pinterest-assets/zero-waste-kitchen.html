Zero Waste Kitchen Guide

Reduce food waste at home with simple composting swaps

🗑️ The Food Waste Problem

The average household throws away 30% of the food they buy. That's about $1,500/year wasted — and it ends up in landfills producing methane.

🍎

Fruit/Veg Scraps

Potato peels, carrot tops, wilted lettuce — compost these!

☕

Coffee & Tea

Groinds and tea bags are nitrogen-rich compost gold

🥚

Eggshells

Crush fine — adds calcium and grit for worms

Kitchen ScrapCompost Method
Coffee groundsWorm bin or hot compost pile
Fruit peelsWorm bin (small amounts)
Vegetable scrapsHot composting (best for large volumes)
Bread/grainsHot composting only (attracts pests in worm bins)
EggshellsAll methods — crush first!

Savings Tip: Composting just your kitchen scraps can save a household $50–100/year on fertilizer for gardens.

🔄 Easy Zero-Waste Kitchen Swaps

✅ Replace plastic wrap with beeswax wraps or reusable containers
✅ Use a compost bin under the sink for daily scraps
✅ Buy loose produce instead of pre-packaged
✅ Freeze excess herbs in oil or water cubes
✅ Save vegetable scraps in a freezer bag for stock

📦 What to Compost vs. Trash

Compost ✅Trash 🗑️
Coffee grounds, tea bagsStyrofoam, plastic bags
Fruit & veg scrapsGlass, metal cans
Eggshells (crushed)Certified compostable plastics*
Dry leaves, cardboardTreated wood chips

*Certified compostable plastics require industrial facilities — not home compost bins.
