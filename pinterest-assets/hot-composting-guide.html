Hot Composting in 2 Weeks

The fast method that turns scraps into rich soil — no worms needed!

🔥 What Is Hot Composting?

A thermophilic process where a large pile of organic matter heats up to 130–160°F (54–71°C), killing weed seeds and pathogens while breaking down materials rapidly.

⏱️

Time

2–6 weeks vs. 3–12 months for cold composting

🌡️

Temperature

Reaches 130–160°F (54–71°C) naturally through microbial activity

StepAction
1. Build pileLayer greens + browns (3:1 ratio), at least 3x3x3 ft
2. Add waterPile should be moist like a wrung-out sponge
3. Monitor heatUse a compost thermometer; turn when temp drops below 100°F
4. Turn regularlyAerate every 5–7 days to maintain oxygen supply

Pro Tip: The bigger the pile, the faster it heats up. A minimum of 3x3x3 feet is needed to retain enough heat.

📈 Heat Timeline

Day 1–3
85°F
Day 4–7
130°F
Day 8–14
160°F 🔥
Day 15+
Cooling → Ready!

Turn the pile when temperature drops below 100°F to re-activate microbial activity.

🧱 Best Materials for Hot Composting

  • Greens (Nitrogen): Grass clippings, vegetable scraps, coffee grounds, manure from herbivores
  • Browns (Carbon): Dry leaves, straw, sawdust, wood chips, shredded cardboard

Golden Ratio: 3 parts browns to 1 part greens by volume. Too many greens = smelly pile.
