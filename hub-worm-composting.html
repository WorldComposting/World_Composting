Troubleshooting Common Problems

If your worm bin is acting up, these are the most common issues and how to fix them.

  • → Fruit flies swarming around the bin — causes and fixes
  • → Bad smell from worm bin — usually means overfeeding or too much moisture
  • → Worms trying to escape through ventilation holes — pH imbalance or temperature issue
  • → Food sitting on top without breaking down — check moisture and worm population

Need more help? Check out the full Troubleshooting Hub for a complete list of common worm bin problems and solutions.

Quick Reference: Worm Composting Basics

TopicKey Point
Best wormsRed Wigglers (Eisenia fetida) — not earthworms from the garden
Bin sizeStart with 2 sq ft of surface area per person in your household
Feeding rateAbout half a pound of scraps per week for every pound of worms
Bedding depth4–6 inches of moist, shredded material (newspaper, coconut coir, leaves)
Harvest cycleEvery 3–6 months depending on bin size and worm population
