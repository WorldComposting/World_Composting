Troubleshooting Common Problems

If your tumbler compost is acting up, these are the most common issues and how to fix them.

  • → Drum smells anaerobic — overloading or insufficient turning frequency
  • → Pile won't heat up — batch too small, wrong C:N ratio, or poor insulation
  • → Leaking from drum seals — bearing wear or gasket failure

Need more help? Check out the full Troubleshooting Hub for a complete list of common tumbler problems and solutions.

Quick Reference: Tumbler Basics

  • Drum capacity:3-7 cubic feet per chamber; dual-chamber allows continuous loading while one cures
  • Turning frequency:Every 2-4 days during active phase; once a week during curing
  • Target temperature:130-150°F (54-66°C) sustained for weed seed destruction
  • Total cycle time:2-8 weeks depending on batch size and ambient temperature
  • Best feedstock:Chopped greens (grass clippings, kitchen scraps) + browns (dry leaves, straw)
