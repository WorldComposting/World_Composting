Troubleshooting Common Problems

If your lasagna compost bed is acting up, these are the most common issues and how to fix them.

  • → Bed smells anaerobic — too wet or insufficient browns between layers
  • → Layers not breaking down — add more greens, water more frequently, or thin thicker brown layers
  • → Weeds growing through cardboard — add a second layer of wet newspaper on top

Need more help? Check out the full Troubleshooting Hub for a complete list of common lasagna problems and solutions.

Quick Reference: Lasagna Basics

  • Layer thickness:3-4 inches greens, 6-8 inches browns (cardboard) per cycle; total bed depth 12-24 inches
  • Decomposition time:3-6 months for full breakdown before planting; can plant on top after 2-3 months
  • Moisture target:Damp sponge — squeeze a handful and only 1-2 drops should come out
  • C:N ratio target:~30:1 by weight; roughly 2 parts browns to 1 part greens by volume per layer
  • Best season to build:Fall (leaves available) or early spring; avoid building in peak summer heat without heavy watering
