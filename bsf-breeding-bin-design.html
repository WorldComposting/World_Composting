BSF Breeding Bin Design: Build Your Own at Home

Design and build your own BSF breeding bin. Learn the best materials, sizing, ventilation, and ramp design.

Contents

  • Choosing Bin Materials
  • Ventilation Design
  • Ramp Construction
  • Egg-Laying Area Setup

Choosing Bin Materials

I use a 5-gallon food-grade bucket for my breeding bin. The key is choosing a container that is easy to clean and has smooth interior walls so larvae can climb out easily.

Ventilation Design

  • Cut a mesh screen into the lid to allow adult flies in but keep predators out
  • Add small air holes along the sides for airflow — too much ventilation dries out the food waste

Ramp Construction

A sloped ramp or cone inside the bin gives larvae a path to climb up and exit. I use a PVC pipe cut at an angle, secured with hot glue.

Egg-Laying Area Setup

  1. Place a small tray of overripe fruit near the bin entrance to attract egg-laying females
  2. Replace every 3-4 days with fresh bait
