Quick Reference: Bokashi Basics

  • Fermentation time:2-4 weeks in the bucket before burying in soil
  • Soil burial wait:2-6 weeks after burying before planting (acidic)
  • Bran ratio:1 handful per 2 inches of food scraps by volume
  • Tea dilution:1 teaspoon per gallon of water (1:1000) for plants
  • Smell test:Healthy = pickles/sourdough; Unhealthy = rotten egg/putrid
