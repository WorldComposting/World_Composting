What is Compost Troubleshooting?

Every composting system encounters problems — odors, pests, slow decomposition, moisture imbalances. The difference between a struggling pile and a thriving one often comes down to knowing how to diagnose the root cause quickly. This troubleshooting guide walks you through the most common issues across all composting methods (bins, piles, tumblers, worm farms) and gives you actionable fixes for each problem.

How to Use This Guide

  • Identify Your Symptom — Start with the phase that matches what you're seeing: bad smells, flies, wet or dry pile, slow breakdown, heat issues, or pests.
  • Follow the Checklist — Each item gives you a specific problem and its solution. Work through them in order until your issue is resolved.
  • Prevent Recurrence — Most problems are preventable with proper balance, moisture management, and regular turning. Use these fixes to adjust your routine going forward.
