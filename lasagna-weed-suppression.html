Lasagna Compost for Weed Suppression: Smother Weeds Naturally

Use lasagna composting to smother weeds naturally. Learn how cardboard layers and thick organic mulch suppress weed growth without herbicides.

Contents

  • Cardboard as Weed Barrier
  • Mulch Depth for Suppression
  • Handling Perennial Weeds
  • Long-Term Benefits

Cardboard as Weed Barrier

The foundation of any lasagna compost bed is a layer of plain cardboard (no glossy print, no tape). This acts as an impenetrable barrier that smothers existing weeds and grass below.

  • Overlap sheets by at least 6 inches to prevent light from reaching weed crowns
  • Wet the cardboard thoroughly before adding soil layers on top

Mulch Depth for Suppression

The thick organic mulch layers (straw, leaves, compost) built on top of the cardboard create a light-blocking blanket that prevents new weed seeds from germinating.

Handling Perennial Weeds

  • For aggressive perennials like bindweed or quackgrass, add a second layer of cardboard on top of the first for extra insurance.
  • Alternatively, smother the area with black plastic for 4-6 weeks before starting your lasagna bed.

Long-Term Benefits

A well-built lasagna compost bed can suppress weeds for an entire growing season. The rich, loose soil it produces also makes any remaining weeds easier to pull by hand.
