Worm Bin Setup
in 5 Steps

Your complete vermicomposting starter guide

✅ Get Started
1
Choose Your Bin
10-gallon tote or worm bag. Drill 8-10 air holes in lid and sides. Worm bags work great for beginners (no drilling needed).
2
Add Bedding
Shredded cardboard or newspaper. Soak in water, wring out until damp like a sponge. Fill 1/3 of bin with fluffy bedding.
3
Order Your Worms
Red Wigglers (Eisenia fetida). Order from a local bait shop or online. You need about 1 lb of worms to start.

⚠️ Not earthworms! Red wigglers eat their body weight in food daily and thrive in bins.

4
Add Food & Bedding Mix
Bury food scraps under bedding. Start with small amounts (1/2 cup). Always cover with 1 inch of dry bedding to prevent fruit flies.
5
Maintain & Harvest
Feed weekly, harvest every 3-6 months. Move finished compost to one side, add fresh bedding on the other. Worms will migrate.

📥 What You'll Need

📜Red wiggler worms
📐Shredded cardboard
💧Spray bottle (water)
⚙️Drill + 1/8" bit

💡 Pro Tip

Keep your bin in a dark, temperature-stable spot (50-80°F). A closet, garage, or under the sink works perfectly.
