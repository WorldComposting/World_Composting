Troubleshoot any problem in 30 seconds

🔍 Common Problems
👃
Bad smell (rotten, ammonia)
✅ The Fix
Add browns immediately. Mix in shredded cardboard or dried leaves. Turn the pile to aerate.
🥶
Not heating up
✅ The Fix
Add greens + moisture. Mix in grass clippings or coffee grounds. Pile must be at least 3ft x 3ft.
💧
Too wet / soggy
✅ The Fix
Add dry browns. Mix in cardboard, sawdust, or leaves. Turn to distribute moisture evenly.
🐦
Pests (rats, flies)
✅ The Fix
Bury food waste deep. Cover scraps with 6+ inches of browns or finished compost. Use a bin with lid.
🐎
Too dry / no breakdown
✅ The Fix
Add water + turn. Pile should feel like a wrung-out sponge. Mix thoroughly after watering.

🔍 Quick Diagnosis

💧 Too wet?
Add browns
☃️ Not heating?
Add greens + water
👃 Smelly?
Too much green, add browns
🦍 Too dry?
Add water + turn pile
