What Goes in a
Worm Bin?

The complete green & brown guide

🌱 Greens (Nitrogen-Rich)
💀
Kitchen ScrapsFruit & veggie peels, coffee grounds, tea bags
🌲
Fresh Grass ClippingsIn thin layers (avoid matting)
🍀
Fresh LeavesYoung, tender leaves from garden
🌻
Plant TrimmingsNon-diseased, non-seeded plants
🍂 Browns (Carbon-Rich)
📐
Shredded CardboardNatural brown cardboard, no glossy print
🍃
Dried LeavesCrisp autumn leaves, crumbled
📝
Newspaper / PaperBlack ink only, shredded into strips
🌰
SawdustFrom untreated wood (small amounts)

⛔ Avoid These

Meat & fish Dairy products Oily foods Pet waste Glossy paper Spicy foods

⚡ Golden Rule: Aim for 3 parts brown to every 1 part green by volume.
