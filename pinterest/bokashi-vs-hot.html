Bokashi vs Hot
Composting

Which method is right for you?

⚔️ Side by Side ⚔️
🏦 Bokashi
Where?
Indoors, garage, apartment
Time
2 weeks to ferment + 4 weeks to cure
What goes in?
ALL food waste including meat, dairy, bones
Smell?
Pickled / sour (fermenting)
Cost
$25 bucket + ongoing bran cost
⚔️
🔥 Hot Pile
Where?
Outdoors, yard, garden area
Time
1-3 months (with turning)
What goes in?
Greens + browns only, no meat/dairy
Smell?
Earthy (when balanced)
Cost
$0-50 bin, free materials

✅ Bokashi is best if you...

• Live in an apartment or have no yard
• Want to compost year-round indoors
• Don't mind buying bran regularly

✅ Hot Composting is best if you...

• Have outdoor space
• Produce lots of yard waste
• Want free, high-volume compost
