  • → Too much ash in final product — incomplete combustion, check airflow

Quick Reference: BioChar Basics

  • Pyrolysis temp range:400-700°F (200-370°C) for stable carbon; above 900°F destroys pore structure
  • Pore size target:Micro-pores (<2nm) for water retention, macro-pores (>50nm) for air and microbial habitat
  • CEC boost:Biochar can increase soil CEC by 10-30% depending on feedstock
  • Charging time:Minimum 2 weeks in compost tea or worm castings before soil application
  • Carbon sequestration:Biochar remains stable for hundreds to thousands of years — a true carbon sink
