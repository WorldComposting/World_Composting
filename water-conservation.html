Why Water Conservation Matters

The average American uses 80-100 gallons of water per day indoors. Outdoors, lawns and gardens can consume up to 60% of household water in dry climates. This checklist walks you through actionable steps from simple daily swaps to bigger system changes.

The Composting Connection

Adding compost to soil increases its water-holding capacity dramatically. Organic matter acts like a sponge, reducing the need for frequent irrigation while also improving drainage and preventing runoff. Every step here ties back to building healthier soil.
